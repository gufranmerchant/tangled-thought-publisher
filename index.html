Privacy Policy — Tangled Thought Publisher

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What this app does

Tangled Thought Publisher is an internal publishing tool used solely by the Tangled Thought team to post our own short-form psychology videos directly to our own TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data we collect

TikTok account authorization: when we connect our TikTok account, TikTok issues an access token and refresh token. These tokens are stored securely on our own server and used only to upload and publish videos we create.

Basic account info: we request the user.info.basic scope, which lets the app confirm which TikTok account is connected. No other personal data is accessed or stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from anyone else — the app has no public users.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking, or advertising through this app.

Data security

Tokens are stored on our server with restricted access and are never exposed publicly. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings.
