Privacy Policy — Tangled Thought Publisher

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What this app does

Tangled Thought Publisher is a publishing tool for short-form video creators. Creators connect their TikTok account through TikTok Login, and the app uploads and publishes the creator's own videos directly to their TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API. Nothing is posted without the creator's action.

Data we collect

TikTok account authorization: when you connect your TikTok account, TikTok issues an access token and refresh token. These tokens are stored securely on our server and used only to upload and publish videos you choose to publish.

Basic account info: we request the user.info.basic scope, which lets the app confirm which TikTok account is connected. No other personal data is accessed or stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking, or advertising through this app.
  • We never publish anything to your account except videos you explicitly choose to publish.

Data security and deletion

Tokens are stored on our server with restricted access and are never exposed publicly. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately stops all access. To request deletion of your stored tokens and data, email us at the address below.
